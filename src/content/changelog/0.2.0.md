### New
- **Rules.** Settings › Rules files new screenshots for you. Each rule reads as a sentence, like "When text mentions receipt → move to Receipts". Rules can move a screenshot to a folder, add a tag, or move it to the Trash after a number of days. Under each rule you can see how many screenshots it matches today. "Apply to these…" files the screenshots you already have, after you review the list, and Undo all puts them back.
- **Smart folders.** The Library sidebar has "New smart folder", which uses the same sentence editor as rules. A folder names itself after its conditions, and it also shows up as a filter in ⌥ Space.
- **Duplicates and retakes.** Library › Duplicates finds exact copies and retakes (almost the same screenshot, taken within 10 minutes). Each set has one screenshot picked to keep. ⌘↩ keeps that one and moves the others to the Trash, and ⌘Z undoes it.
- **Spotlight.** ⌘ Space now finds screenshots by the words inside them. Screenshots that contain secrets are never added to Spotlight. You can turn this off in Settings, and turning it off removes everything Shotlight added.
- **Finder tags** (off by default). Shotlight can add Code, Receipt and rule tags to the files. It never removes tags you added yourself.
- **Better screen recordings.** Shotlight now reads 4 frames across a recording instead of 1. It can also transcribe what was said, on your Mac, if macOS already has a speech model installed (it never downloads one). Search by what was said, and the preview starts playing at that moment. In the Library, move the pointer across a recording to look through it.
- **Learns from your edits.** When you rename a screenshot, Shotlight remembers the new name. After three edits that agree, it asks once, for example "You usually drop the app name. Always?". Settings › Naming lists everything it learned, and you can remove any of it. It keeps only the names you typed, never the text on the screen.
- **A weekly line in the menu.** "This week · 14 filed · 6 trashed · 3 duplicates removed". Click a number to see those screenshots. Shotlight shows no notifications.

### Fixed
- Previewing a screen recording in ⌥ Space no longer shows a spinner that never stops.
