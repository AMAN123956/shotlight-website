- **Named the moment you take it.** New screenshots and screen recordings get a clear name from the text inside them, with Undo in the little pill that appears.
- **⌥ Space to find any screenshot** by the words in it, with filters, previews and keyboard shortcuts for everything.
- **Tidy Up** renames the screenshots you already have, after you review every name, with one Undo all.
- **Library** window: browse by day or app, find duplicates, save searches.
- **Sensitive guard**: screenshots with passwords, keys or card numbers are blurred and kept out of search. One-time codes can move to the Trash on their own after a few days.
- Names your way: short, standard or detailed, with or without app and date, your separator and letter case.
