### Fixed
- **Guard catches keys that the text reader misreads.** Apple's text recognition sometimes reads a letter in a key or token as a lookalike from another alphabet, such as a Cyrillic "х" in place of an "x", most often in monospaced text. Guard then missed the key, or blanked only part of it. It now reads those lookalikes as the plain letters, so the whole key is flagged, blanked and kept out of names, search and Spotlight. This applies to screenshots Shotlight reads from this version on.
