### Better names
- **Web pages are named after the page, not the site.** A GitHub repository is named after the repository ("localkit, GitHub") and a product page after the product ("Apple HomeView"), instead of "GitHub page" or a stray line of text from the page.
- **Overview screens are named after their title.** A design canvas, board or long document with several sections of the same size is named from the title at the top of the window ("ClearSpend - High-fidelity UI"), not from its first section.
- **Section numbers are left out.** "2 · Capture" or "02: Capture" becomes "Capture". Numbers that belong to the name, like "2024 tax return" or "1.5x speed", stay.

### Fixed
- "UI" no longer comes out as "Ul" when the text reader misreads the capital I.
- Names no longer end in a needless "page" ("Apple HomeView page" → "Apple HomeView").
